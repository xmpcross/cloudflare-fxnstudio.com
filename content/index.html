How we work

A structured process keeps every project focused, transparent and moving in the right direction.

Share what you want to build

We start by understanding your business, audience, goals, challenges and current digital systems. This helps us identify what needs to be built and why it matters.

We define the project scope, user journeys, content structure, technical requirements and recommended platforms. This stage creates a practical roadmap for design and development.

Development and integration

Clean code, integration-ready architecture

We build the website, application or platform using modern technology, clean code, responsive layouts and integration-ready architecture.

Testing, launch and support

Test, launch and keep improving

We test across devices, browsers, forms, workflows and important user actions to make sure the final product works as expected.

We prepare the site or system for release, support deployment and help ensure tracking, performance and essential SEO elements are in place.

After launch, we can continue helping with updates, optimisation, new features, content changes and technical support.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can’t find what you need? Get in touch.

Who do you work with?

Businesses of all sizes, including startups, local companies, growing brands, professional service firms, e-commerce businesses, agencies and global organisations.

Do you work with clients outside Australia?

Yes. FXN Studio is established in Australia, but our services are available to clients worldwide. We work remotely with teams across time zones using clear communication, structured milestones and flexible delivery models.

What does your process look like?

Eight stages: discovery, strategy, design, development, integration, testing, launch, and support and improvement. Each stage has a clear purpose.

Do you build with open-source technology?

Yes. We use modern open-source technologies and trusted platforms, which helps reduce unnecessary lock-in and gives businesses room to scale.

Can you help after launch?

Yes. After launch we can continue helping with updates, optimisation, new features, content changes and technical support.
