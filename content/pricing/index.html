Engagement questions

How do you scope a project?

We start with discovery to understand your business, audience, goals, challenges and current digital systems. We then define the scope, user journeys, content structure and technical requirements, so you have a practical roadmap before design and development begin.

Are these fixed prices?

No. They are the estimated budget bands from our project brief. Every project is scoped individually, so your recommendation depends on your goals and requirements.

How long does a project take?

A typical build moves from discovery and strategy in week 1, to design and prototyping in weeks 2–3, and build and integration in weeks 3–6, with ongoing support after launch. Timing varies by project.

Do you support the project after launch?

Yes. After launch we can continue helping with updates, optimisation, new features, content changes and technical support.

Do you work with clients outside Australia?

Yes. FXN Studio is based in Australia and our services are available to Australian and global clients of any size.
